Everything plain Mermaid can't do

Mermaid draws a fixed picture. Untangle keeps the same code underneath and makes the picture something you can work in.

Drag it into shape

Move boxes, bend lines around each other, reorder sequence messages and change who sends them.

Edit where you look

Double-click any label to fix it. Add tables, steps and participants without touching code.

Walk through it

Step through a flowchart one decision at a time, or play a sequence diagram message by message.

Share and export

Send a view-only link, or export print-quality PNG and SVG in light, dark or transparent.

Works with the Mermaid you already have

Upload a .mmd file or paste code. Your layout is saved as comments that Mermaid ignores, so the file still works on GitHub, GitLab and Notion.

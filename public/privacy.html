Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Untangle is a diagram editor. This page says what it stores about you, why, and how to remove it.

What we store

  • Your account: your name, your email address and your password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody can read it.
  • Your diagrams: their titles, their Mermaid code, a preview image, and up to 50 earlier versions of each so you can restore them.
  • A login cookie: one cookie that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It is required for the app to work. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Server logs: the time, the page or action, and your account id for each request, kept to find faults and stop abuse.

If you use the editor without an account, your diagram stays in your own browser and is not sent to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads, and we don't use analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We don't sell or share your data.
  • Fonts, scripts and the diagram library are served from this site, so loading a page contacts no other company.

Who else handles your data

  • The hosting company that runs our server stores the data on our behalf.
  • An email delivery company sends password reset emails, so it sees your email address when you ask for one.

Sharing

Your diagrams are private. If you turn on a share link for a diagram, anyone who has that link can see the diagram and your name. Turning sharing off makes the link stop working.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Account settings has a button that downloads all your diagrams.
  • Delete your data: deleting a diagram removes it and its versions. Deleting your account in Account settings removes your account, every diagram and every share link straight away. Copies in our routine backups are overwritten as those backups expire.
  • Correct your data: you can change your name in Account settings.
